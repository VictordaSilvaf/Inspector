# Design

## Visual theme

Instagram-adjacent social polish: near-black surfaces, bright warm accent (coral/magenta), story rings, full-bleed product moments. Not purple-indigo SaaS.

## Colors (OKLCH)

- `--ink`: oklch(0.18 0.02 40) — near-black warm
- `--paper`: oklch(0.99 0.005 90) — clean off-white
- `--accent`: oklch(0.62 0.22 25) — coral/instagram-warm
- `--accent-2`: oklch(0.55 0.24 350) — magenta for rings only
- `--muted`: oklch(0.55 0.02 40)

## Typography

- Display / UI: **Sora** (weights 400–700)
- Avoid Instrument Sans on the marketing surface only; app shell may keep existing tokens.

## Motion

Motion/react: fade-up, story ring rotate, soft parallax. Reduced-motion = opacity only.

## Components (landing)

No card grids of icon+title. Prefer full-bleed bands, phone mock frames, story-strip CTAs.
